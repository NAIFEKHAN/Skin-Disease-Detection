AI Skin Disease Detection

About the Project

This application helps analyse skin lesion images using AI. It detects the lesion area, predicts the possible skin condition, shows an attention map, and gives an estimated treatment cost for educational use.

How It Works in 4 Steps

Analysis Workflow
1
Upload Image
User uploads or takes a photo of the skin lesion. Quality check ensures clear lighting.
2
Detect Lesion
U-Net model finds the exact lesion boundaries and calculates relative area coverage.
3
Predict Disease
Vision Transformer analyses the image patches to predict the disease among 7 categories.
4
View Report
Shows attention heatmap, care advice, estimated cost in INR, and downloadable PDF.

How the AI Works

Simple Breakdown
🔬 Image Preprocessing
Checks image resolution, blur, and lighting using OpenCV. Applies DullRazor hair-removal filter when fine skin hair covers the lesion.
🎯 Lesion Segmentation
Runs a ResNet18 U-Net neural network to mark the lesion contour and calculate what percentage of the image is covered by the lesion.
🧠 Disease Classification
Uses a Vision Transformer (ViT-B/16) to calculate disease probabilities and rank the top three matching skin conditions.
🔥 Attention Map
Traces self-attention weights across all 12 transformer layers so users can see which part of the skin influenced the prediction.
📄 Report Generation
Combines the 4 image views, estimated Indian medical cost breakdown (INR), and educational notes into a clean report and PDF.

Dual Analysis Architecture

System Expansion

The platform is architected to support two distinct dermatological modalities with strict boundary isolation between dermoscopy and ambient clinical photography:

🔬 Mode 1: Dermoscopic Lesions ACTIVE

Specialized for dermatoscope-magnified pigmented lesions across 7 HAM10000 categories (Melanoma, Nevi, BCC, AKIEC, BKL, DF, VASC).

  • ResNet18 U-Net segmentation (0.70 threshold)
  • ViT-B/16 classifier (64.59% accuracy, 68.68% F1)
  • Full multi-modal PDF report & 4-view breakdown
📱 Mode 2: Common Skin Conditions PENDING APPROVAL

Designed for ambient clinical smartphone photography across 9 categories (Acne, Eczema, Psoriasis, Impetigo, Ringworm, Scabies, Urticaria, etc.).

  • ViT-B/16 classifier (28.09% test accuracy, 26.89% F1)
  • Dermoscopic U-Net segmentation bypassed (not validated on non-dermoscopic ambient photos)
  • Kept disabled pending clinical review & explicit approval

Known Limitations

Academic Boundaries
  • AI prediction may be wrong: Image predictions are computerized guesses and can make errors.
  • Mobile camera images reduce reliability: The operational AI was trained on dermoscopic lenses; smartphone photos with glare or low lighting may give inaccurate outputs.
  • Dermoscopic vs Ambient Separation: Dermoscopic models must not be used on ambient smartphone photos without clinical domain adaptation.
  • Common Skin Model Scarcity: The experimental common skin model achieves 26.89% Macro F1 due to strong visual overlap between inflammatory dermatoses (eczema, urticaria, psoriasis).
  • Melanoma detection is limited: Melanoma recall is 47.73%, meaning early-stage cases can be missed and need in-person professional review.
  • Treatment costs are estimates: Cost ranges in INR are benchmark guide values for Indian clinics, not hospital bills or quotes.

For educational use only. Not a medical diagnosis. Consult a qualified dermatologist.
